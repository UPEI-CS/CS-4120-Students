One candidate, five fitted hypothesis functions

A candidate is a configuration. Five-fold cross-validation trains that configuration five times, each time on a different training subset.

Teal blocks: training. Purple block: validation. Every row is validated once and used for training in four fits.

Fixed synthetic data. Target y = 2 + x + 0.2x² plus a fixed small disturbance. Fold groups are interleaved across x.

MSE is mean squared prediction error. Each fold learns its own coefficients using only training rows. Comparing two candidates requires 10 CV fits. After selection, fitting once on all development data is an additional fit. A held-out test set is outside this demonstration.
