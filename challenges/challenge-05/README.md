# Challenge 5 · TraCE reflection

[Course challenges](../README.md)

This is a separate reflection on your work in Challenge 4. No additional model run is required.

## One reflection question

Choose one conclusion you reached in Challenge 4 about model choice or a feature explanation. How did using TraCE help you examine that conclusion, and what can you now defend with evidence?

Write one connected reflection that makes your thinking visible: your initial view (Think), the question you asked TraCE (Request), what you questioned or accepted in its response (Assess), the lab evidence you used to verify it (Check), and your final explanation in your own words, including what remains uncertain (Explain).

A conclusion that stays the same is valid if your checks support it. Do not submit a chat transcript or let TraCE write the reflection for you.

## Submission

Use [the Word reflection template](Challenge-5%20TraCE%20Reflection.docx). Include your name or group and student IDs, and write one connected reflection in your own words. Refer to concrete lab evidence rather than listing the TraCE steps without showing how you used them. Save your completed Word file and submit it to the Challenge 5 Moodle assignment.

The question and submission text above can be copied directly into Moodle. Deadlines and grading settings are set in Moodle by the instructor.
